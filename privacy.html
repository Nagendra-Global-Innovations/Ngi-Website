Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This is the website of Nagendra Global Innovations (NGI), founded and led by Mudunuru Avinash Varma, Founder and CEO. This policy explains what happens to information when you use nagendraglobalinnovations.in.

What we collect

  • This website has no sign-up, no accounts and no forms. We do not ask you to enter personal details here.
  • If you email us (for example contact@, support@, ceo@ or founder@), we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation.
  • Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the website.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising and do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not set tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not run analytics that follow you across sites.

Links to other sites

This website links to FaculZo (faculzo.nagendraglobalinnovations.in), Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, GitHub and the founder’s personal website. They have their own privacy policies. FaculZo has its own policy at faculzo.nagendraglobalinnovations.in/privacy.html.

Keeping and deleting your information

Emails are kept as long as needed to deal with your request. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email contact@nagendraglobalinnovations.in.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears at the top.
