Founder & CEO

Mudunuru Avinash Varma

Founder and CEO, Nagendra Global Innovations

Mudunuru Avinash Varma (also written Avinash Varma Mudunuru, Avinash Mudunuru or Avinash Varma) is the Founder and CEO of Nagendra Global Innovations (NGI), a technology company based in Andhra Pradesh, India.

NGI builds software, AI-powered tools and future-ready solutions for people and organisations around the world. Its products are FaculZo, a set of connected campus apps for students, staff and security, and NGI Vision Connect Pro, which is coming soon.

Avinash focuses on connecting AI systems, productivity with AI tools and building clean, useful interfaces. His belief is simple.

“We don’t build technology to predict the future; we build it to make the old way of working obsolete.”Mudunuru Avinash Varma, Founder and CEO
